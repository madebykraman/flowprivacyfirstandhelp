Privacy

Your data. Your choice.

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Private tracker

The private tracker stores profile settings, period logs, symptoms, notes and cycle history in your browser's IndexedDB storage. No account is required. NijRitu does not need your cycle history, symptoms or notes on a health-data backend.

Backups

Exports are created on your device. Encrypted backups use Web Crypto AES-GCM with a passphrase-derived key. If you explicitly use the optional relay, it receives only encrypted ciphertext plus a random backup identifier and stores a hash of the separate access key. Relay backups expire by default after 90 days.

User-controlled destinations

You can use a local export, encrypted export, or a user-selected WebDAV / Nextcloud destination. WebDAV credentials are entered for the operation and are not stored by NijRitu. Provider CORS and security policies can affect direct access.

Browser storage is not permanent

Browsers can clear site data. Persistent storage may be requested where supported, but NijRitu cannot guarantee retention. Keep a backup when the data matters.

Community

The public community is separate from private tracking. Posts and replies are moderated before publication. Application-level anonymity does not mean network-level anonymity. Technical metadata such as IP addresses may exist in underlying infrastructure. Do not publish names, addresses, medical records or other identifying information.

Community Pro

Professional directory listings are public information submitted by professionals. Listings are separate from private tracker data and are reviewed before publication. Verification means submitted professional credentials or evidence were reviewed; it is not a guarantee of treatment quality, availability or outcome.

External sources

Knowledge links open third-party publishers such as NHS, ACOG and WHO. Their privacy policies and practices apply when you leave NijRitu.

Medical boundary

NijRitu is a tracking and information utility, not a medical device, diagnostic service or contraception tool. Predictions are estimates and are not medical certainty.
