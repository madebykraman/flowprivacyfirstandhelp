Privacy

Your data. Your choice.

Last updated: 4 September 2026

What NijRitu stores

The private tracker stores profile settings, period logs, symptoms, notes and cycle history in your browser's IndexedDB storage. NijRitu does not require an account for private tracking.

What NijRitu does not receive

Private cycle history, symptoms and notes are not sent to a NijRitu health-data backend. There is no advertising SDK or analytics SDK in the application.

Backups

Exports are created on your device. Encrypted backups use Web Crypto AES-GCM with a passphrase-derived key. The passphrase is not stored by NijRitu and cannot be recovered if forgotten.

Remote backups

If you explicitly choose WebDAV or Nextcloud, NijRitu encrypts the backup in your browser before uploading it. If you explicitly choose the optional NijRitu relay, it receives opaque ciphertext only. Relay backup access requires a separate access key and backups expire by default after 90 days. The relay does not receive your passphrase.

Browser storage is not permanent

Browsers can clear site data. Persistent storage may be requested where supported, but NijRitu cannot guarantee retention. Keep a backup when the data matters.

Community

The optional public community is separate from private tracking. Submissions and replies contain only what a person chooses to publish and are held for moderation before public display. Application-level anonymity does not mean network infrastructure cannot observe technical metadata such as an IP address or timestamp. Do not publish names, contact details, medical records or other identifying information.

External sources

Knowledge links open third-party publishers such as NHS, ACOG and WHO. Their privacy policies and practices apply when you leave NijRitu.

Medical boundary

NijRitu is a tracking and information utility, not a medical device, diagnostic service or contraception tool. Predictions are estimates and are not medical certainty.
