Privacy

Your data. Your choice.

Last updated: 4 September 2026

What NijRitu stores

The private tracker stores profile settings, period logs, symptoms, notes and cycle history in your browser's IndexedDB storage. NijRitu does not require an account for the private tracker.

What NijRitu does not receive

The V1.0 private tracker does not send your cycle history, symptoms or notes to a NijRitu health-data backend. There is no advertising SDK or analytics SDK in the launch build.

Backups

Exports are created on your device. Encrypted backups use Web Crypto AES-GCM with a passphrase-derived key. NijRitu does not store your passphrase and cannot recover an encrypted backup if it is forgotten.

Browser storage is not permanent

Browsers can clear site data. Persistent storage may be requested where supported, but NijRitu cannot guarantee retention. Keep a backup when the data matters.

External sources

Knowledge links open third-party publishers such as NHS, ACOG and WHO. Their privacy policies and practices apply when you leave NijRitu.

Public features

The V1.0 build does not operate a server-backed anonymous community or professional directory. Those features remain separate until their technical privacy and moderation model is ready to publish.

Medical boundary

NijRitu is a tracking and information utility, not a medical device, diagnostic service or contraception tool.
